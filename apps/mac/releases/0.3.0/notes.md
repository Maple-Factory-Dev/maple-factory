### Added

- **Native session view**: watch an agent's session as a readable conversation, with its messages, tool calls, diffs and prompts, and type to it from there. A **Native**/**Terminal** switch flips back to the terminal at any time.
- **Request plan approval from** and **Request review from**: ask a teammate to approve a plan or review a change, like a review request on GitHub.
- **Time**: each task shows how long it spent in total, and how much of that went to waiting, planning, building and reviewing.
- **Open Large** shows an HTML mockup or artifact big, at Desktop, Tablet or Mobile width.
- **Check for Updates…** has a shortcut: **⇧⌘U**.
- A builder can mark a **Done when** item as done for a person once they confirm it in an answer.

### Changed

- The online facepile is gone from the factory's top bar.
- In a new task's brief, **Shift-Tab** moves back to the title.
- The usage card names the Fable weekly limit properly, instead of showing "7d:fable".

### Fixed

- The live status and session view show the answer you sent, instead of a `<pasted_content …>` tag.
