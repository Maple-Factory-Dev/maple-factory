### Added

- **Why this risk** on a task lists every reason behind its risk level: the planner's read, each risk check that matched (with the files it matched), the change's size, and any risk the goal accepted.
- **Risk checks** in the risk policy editor take plain-language rules, such as "changes the public HTTP API", next to path checks. Use **Add check** to add one. The planner cites each rule that applies.
