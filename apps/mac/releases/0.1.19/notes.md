### Added

- More than one factory: the app remembers every factory you sign in to. Switch between them from the factory menu at the top of the sidebar, or the collapsed rail, add another with **Add new factory…**, or sign out of one.
- The task's **⋯** menu does more: next steps, Rename…, teammates' machines, Open pull request, Copy branch name and Copy ID, on merged tasks too. Board cards get Rename… as well.
- When an update is ready, the sidebar shows a **What's new** link beside the version.
