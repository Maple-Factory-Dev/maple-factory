### Added

- A queue for tasks: the board's Queued column numbers them in the order they'll start. Drag to reorder, Move to top or bottom, Schedule… one for later, or Start now.
- Updates show at the bottom of the sidebar, with their progress (a ring in the collapsed rail), instead of in a separate window.

### Changed

- Maple Factory checks for updates every hour.
