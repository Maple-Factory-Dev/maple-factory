### Added

- Cancel or retry a task in any state that isn't merged, from the **⋯** menu in the task panel and full view, or by right-clicking a task. Cancel asks first and says what it will stop.

### Changed

- Retry restarts a running agent, runs the review again, or picks a cancelled task back up and reopens its pull request.
