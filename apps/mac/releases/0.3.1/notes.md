### Added

- **Pause New Work** on a machine: the tasks already on it finish planning, building and review, but nothing new starts there.

### Changed

- A goal's **Follow** is a star by its name, and the goal header has fewer buttons on the right. The rest, such as **Risk Policy…**, are under **More goal actions**.
- **People** in the sidebar have square avatars, a muted "you", and a card with their details when you hover over one.
- The **About** window's note is signed with the full afomera.dev logo, in light and dark.
