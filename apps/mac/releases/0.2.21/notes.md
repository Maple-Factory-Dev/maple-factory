### Added

- **Quiet hours** in **Settings › Notifications**: a weekly schedule in your time zone when nothing notifies you, on any of your devices. The sidebar shows **Quiet hours until** while one is on.
- A **Task budget** in the factory's settings: **Agent time per task** (an hour unless you change it) and **Cost per task**. Past either, the agent is asked to raise a hand, and the factory raises one for it if it doesn't within 10 minutes.
- Teammates who only have the iPhone or Android app open show a phone badge and **Online on mobile**.
