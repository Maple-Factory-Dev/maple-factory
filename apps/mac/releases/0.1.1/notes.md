### Added

- Draft with AI has **Watch**: see what the agent is saying and reading as it drafts, and look back once it's done. Closing the composer leaves a draft running, and New Task comes back to it.
- Attachments: paste or drop a file into a brief or note to upload it to the factory and link to it. Preview shows attached images, and agents get the files too.
- Comments, change requests and the discussion render Markdown, with highlighted code.

### Changed

- Approve changes waits for the automatic review: it reads **Approve after review** until the review passes, and the review panel shows where it's at.
- Approve plan, approve changes and merge show that they're working, and a second click sends nothing.
