### Added

- The **Changes** tab (formerly **Diff**) reviews a task's whole diff or one commit at a time: pick a commit with ‹ ›, read its message, and see only its files.
- **Settings › General › Privacy** has **Send crash and error reports**. Turning it off stops reports straight away.
- Planners and builders can propose tasks they find. Each one lands as a draft in its goal, for you to send to planning or delete.
