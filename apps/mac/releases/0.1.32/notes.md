### Added

- **Settings › Merging:** turn off *Merge approved changes automatically*, and nothing merges on its own. Approved, proven changes wait in a new **Pending merge** column on the board, with a **Merge** button on each card.
- **Goal due dates:** set, move or remove a goal's due date from the goal sheet. Past due reads overdue in red.

### Changed

- A goal with no due date shows none, instead of one made up 21 days out.
- The Risk popover's auto-merge checkboxes say when auto-merge is off for the whole factory.
