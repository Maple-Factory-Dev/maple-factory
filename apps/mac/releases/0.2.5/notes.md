### Added

- A goal's **Overview** sorts its tasks by what to do with them: **Needs you**, **Up next** (drafts and the queue, which you can reorder), **In motion** and **Recently landed**.
- Tasks show their priority (P0 to P3) as a chip on their cards. A follow-up task made from a review finding shows **Follow-up of** with the task it came from.

### Changed

- **Activity** opens quickly even with a long history. It shows 50 entries at a time, with **Show 50 more** for older ones.
