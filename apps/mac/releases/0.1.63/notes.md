### Added

- **Share** on a live session: pick teammates to watch your terminal, or to type in it too. You can stop sharing at any time, and a share ends when the session does.
- **Shared with you** in Machines lists the sessions teammates have shared with you. Watch-only sessions open read-only.
- maplefactory://session links open a shared session straight in the app.

### Changed

- Behind the scenes: the app's connection to your factory now lives in a shared package, the groundwork for an iPhone and iPad app. Nothing changes in how you use it.
