### Added

- Plans can now include **Diagrams**: flows, architectures and state machines the planner draws to explain a change, shown in the task with their notes.
- **Open** shows a diagram large: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and press ⌘F to search it. Esc closes it.

### Changed

- Ships with maple-runner 0.107.0.
