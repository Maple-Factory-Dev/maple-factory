### Added

- **Hand off** for tasks that go over budget: in the risk policy, under **When a task goes over**, choose **Hand off**. Past its time or cost limit, the agent says what's done and what's left. The factory then pushes its branch, files a draft follow-up with the plan, what didn't finish and the budget used, and cancels the task, so a person can decide whether to spend more.
