### Added

- **Efforts**: link tasks into a bigger piece of work. Pick or name an effort from a task's Effort row, see its other tasks in the Effort section, and spot the effort on board cards. Split parts and follow-ups join their task's effort.
- **Settings › Prompts**, for admins: add your own instructions to every agent's prompt. A goal's lead can add some for that goal from the goal sheet's Agent instructions.

### Changed

- A goal's timeline hides merged tasks. Show them with **Show merged · N** in its header, and the app remembers your choice.
