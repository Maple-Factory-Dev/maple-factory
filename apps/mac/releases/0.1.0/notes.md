The first release of Maple Factory for Mac that updates itself.

### Added

- Updates: Maple Factory checks for a new version every 6 hours, or now from **Maple Factory › Check for Updates…**.
- Settings › Updates, to turn automatic checks off or take betas too.
- Settings › Account shows the factory's maple-server version, and when a newer one is out.

### Fixed

- Signing in to a factory welcomes you to it by name, not to the demo's Studio Workspace.
