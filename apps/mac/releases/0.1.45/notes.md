### Added

- **Reorder drafts:** drag drafts on a goal's board to put them in the order you want, or right-click one for **Move to top** and **Move to bottom**.
- **Hide fixed** on Review findings: a chip with its count hides findings that were fixed, and the legend's statuses filter the list.
- **⌘B** shows and hides the sidebar.
