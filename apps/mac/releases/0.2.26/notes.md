### Added

- **Day report** in the sidebar and the ⌘K palette: what you merged, reviewed and have waiting or in progress for a day, ready to copy as Markdown or for Slack.
- An **About Maple Factory** window with the app's version and **Check for Updates…**.

### Changed

- A task's pull request card is redesigned with GitHub's own icons for open, draft, merged and closed.
- The board starts with **Backlog** in the far-left column, then Drafting, Queued and Planning.

### Fixed

- ⌘↩ sends the task comment you're typing, instead of toggling full view and losing it.
