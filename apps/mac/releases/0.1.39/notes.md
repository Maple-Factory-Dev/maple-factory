### Added

- **Findings to tasks:** in Statistics › Review findings, tick findings (or **Select all**) and **Make a task** from them. It opens New Task with a title, a brief that points at each finding's file, and a Done when line per finding. Right-click a finding for the same.

### Changed

- When the task panel is off in Settings, its header button is gone instead of greyed out.
