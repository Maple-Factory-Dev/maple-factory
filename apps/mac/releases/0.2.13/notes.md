### Changed

- Board columns with many tasks load their cards as you scroll. The column header still shows the full count, with **Showing 40 of 160** and **Show all** at the bottom of a long column.
- A runner's **Update** installs the version it showed you. If a newer release came out in the meantime, nothing is installed, and pressing **Update** again installs the newer one.
