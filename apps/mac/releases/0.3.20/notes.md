### Added

- **Docs**: plans, specs and notes your team writes together, edited live with everyone's cursors. Find them under **Docs** in the sidebar, or make one with **New doc**, standing alone or in a goal.
- Goal pages have a **Docs** section listing that goal's docs, with **New doc** right there.
- **⌘K** can make a **New doc** and finds docs by title.
- Docs can hold Mermaid diagrams, tables, charts, images, callouts, toggles and live task embeds: type **/** in a doc to add one.
- Each doc's menu has **Rename**, **Archive** and **Copy link**.

### Fixed

- Mermaid diagrams whose labels hold special characters (like paths such as `/app/docs`) now render instead of failing.
