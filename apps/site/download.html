Maple Factory for Mac.

Your factory’s inbox, plans to approve and changes to review, and the runner that puts this Mac to work. It keeps itself up to date.

Download for macOS

macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.

  1. Install

    Open the disk image and drag Maple Factory to Applications.

  2. Connect to your factory

    Open the app, enter your factory’s address and sign in with GitHub. No factory yet? Deploy one, or look around the demo workspace first.

  3. Add this Mac

    From Machines, add this Mac and it starts taking sessions in the background, starting at login. Your teammates add theirs the same way.

It checks for updates every hour; Maple Factory › Check for Updates… checks now. For prereleases too, choose Beta in Settings › Updates.
